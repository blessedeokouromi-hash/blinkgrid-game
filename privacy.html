⚡ BlinkGrid Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BlinkGrid. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how the game works.

2. Information We Store

BlinkGrid currently uses your browser's local storage to save game information, including your personal best score, daily streak and daily challenge completion status.

This information is stored on your device and is not currently sent to a BlinkGrid account or central player database.

3. Personal Information

BlinkGrid currently does not require registration, an email address, a phone number or a player account.

4. Sharing Your Score

If you choose to use the Share Score feature, your device may open its sharing menu. Information you choose to share is handled by the app or service you select.

5. Advertising and Analytics

BlinkGrid does not currently operate its own advertising or analytics integrations. If these services are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the information they may process.

6. Children's Privacy

BlinkGrid is a general memory game. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

You can clear your browser's site data to remove locally stored game progress. Doing so may permanently erase your saved scores and streaks.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when BlinkGrid adds features or changes how information is handled. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the BlinkGrid team through the official website or project contact channel once one is published.
